about us

We are a chapter of e-NABLE at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. e-NABLE is a global network of volunteers who use 3D printing and emerging technologies to create a more acessible framework for prostehetics. We are a student organization that designs and builds 3D printed prosthetic hands for those in need, with a focus on children. Beyond manufacturing, assembling, and distributing e-NABLE's in-house designs, we hope to push the boundaries of affordable prosthetics by innovating using novel mass market components and materials.
