EOH 2026 Demo

For our EOH 2026 project, we worked on a proof of concept for a low-cost EMG-controlled prosthetic. Though similar projects have been done in the past, we hoped to acquaint our members with basic prosthetic technology including:

  • 3D design and printing
  • laser cutting
  • EMG sensors
  • servomotor control
  • microcontroller programming

We also wanted to emphasize important limitations in existing purely mechanical prosthetic designs (limited applicability due to physical variation in residual limbs, space constraints in providing sufficent power to prosthetics, limited inputs from EMG sensors alone, etc). We hope to work more on the use of other input modailities (e.g. IMUs, camera-assisted control for grasp detection [1] , ToF/IR distance sensors, etc) as well various feedback modalities (e.g. haptic, visual, weak electrostimulation, temperature, etc) in future projects.
